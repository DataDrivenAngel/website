How to play
  • Drag each block onto the 9×9 grid. Blocks can't be rotated.
  • Fill a whole row, column or 3×3 square to clear it.
  • Clear two or more at once for a combo bonus.
  • Clear something on back-to-back moves to build a streak. A move that clears nothing resets it.
  • Empty the whole board for a clean-board bonus.
  • You get three new blocks once all three are placed. The game ends when none of the remaining blocks fit.

Scoring: 1 point per square placed, 18 per line or square cleared, plus combo and streak bonuses.

Installed or not, the game works offline once it has loaded once.

Keyboard: Tab to a block, press Enter to pick it, arrow keys to move, Enter to place, Esc to cancel.
